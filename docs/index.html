Scrollblock

Privacy and support for the experimental network-test build.

Updated November 15, 2026 · Draft for owner review

Current functionality

This build does not yet provide validated Reels blocking. Its optional five-minute network test observes a limited set of Instagram-domain DNS queries. It does not read Instagram messages, passwords, videos, or encrypted request paths.

Network processing

When you explicitly start a test and allow the VPN configuration, system DNS queries for instagram.com and cdninstagram.com are forwarded to Cloudflare over HTTPS. Cloudflare receives these queries and your network address. Other apps using those domains may also be included. Other application traffic is not routed through this test. Cloudflare’s processing is governed by its own resolver privacy commitments.

Diagnostics and sharing

Up to 128 recent hostname observations, timestamps, activity labels and blocking decisions remain in the extension’s memory. They are cleared when the session ends. There is no automatic diagnostic upload or analytics integration in this build. The Share action sends a copy only to the destination you select; saved copies follow that destination’s storage and retention rules.

Your controls

You can stop a test in the app or disconnect/remove its VPN configuration in iOS Settings. Tests stop after five minutes and do not reconnect on demand. No Instagram login, root certificate, or account connection is requested by Scrollblock.
